Dates become things you can tick. `ageBuckets` (the filter keys — nested, because ranges are: something touched today is also within the last 7 days), `ageBucket` ("today", "last 7 days", …), `dueState` ("overdue", "soon", "later", "undated") and `relativeAge` ("6 d ago") turn a timestamp into the words a person filters by — feed them to `meta.facetBuckets` and counts, OR-within, AND-across, the URL and the agent tools work on them unchanged. `parseDateish` reads only shapes whose meaning is not in doubt — ISO (with an optional time and zone), the dotted European `d.m.yyyy`, and year-first `yyyy/m/d` — and validates the calendar day. Everything else is `null`, including `03/04/2026` and `03-04-2026` (March in one country, April in another) and the shorthand a lenient parser invents dates from (`Date.parse("1")` is the year 2001). A field where people write "Contract signature (May)" needs a parser that says no. A day is a CALENDAR day in the reader's own zone throughout: 23:30 last night and 00:30 this morning are a day apart though barely an hour passed, a bare date means that day where the reader is rather than in UTC, and a date-only deadline runs out at the end of THEIR day — not at 17:00 for anyone in UTC−7.
